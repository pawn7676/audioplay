Instructions

Signing in happens on lichess.org, so this site never sees your password. Lichess will ask you to allow this site to do 3 things on your behalf: read challenges, send and accept challenges, and play games. You can revoke this site's access any time under Preferences → Security on Lichess, or by signing out here.

Sign in once and the page waits for a game — one started here, in the Lichess app, or by a friend's challenge. Nothing else to set up.

One tap on the round button starts the microphone and the speech — iOS requires the tap. Then walk to your board: say moves like "echo four", "knight takes delta five", "castle kingside". "repeat" re-speaks the last move, "clock" speaks the times. The "clock" button is a black screen with just the two clocks, side to move drawn heavier, everything else spoken; tap it to come back. "practice" parses moves and answers with a random legal reply, sending nothing. "settings" holds the switches.

The page speaks with the SYSTEM voice. On an iPhone or iPad a much better one is free: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, pick a Premium or Enhanced voice (Ava is a good one) and let it download. Re-download it after an iOS update if the sound gets worse.
