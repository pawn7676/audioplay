Voice

seconds, comma-separated

One tap starts the microphone and the speech — iOS requires the tap. Then walk to your board: say moves like "echo four", "knight takes delta five", "castle kingside". "repeat" re-speaks the last move, "clock" speaks the times, "flip clock" swaps the clock sides. Voice only: nothing on screen is needed — the screen can even be off. Clock: a black screen with just the two clocks, side to move drawn heavier, everything else spoken. Silent: four quadrants — clocks left, last moves right — and nothing spoken at all; questions appear as numbered lists, answered by voice. Both black screens fill the page under Safari's toolbar; tap one to come back here. Practice parses moves and answers with a random legal reply, sending nothing.

Signing in happens on lichess.org, so this site never sees your password. Lichess will ask you to allow three things: read challenges, send and accept challenges, and play games. That is all it can do — it cannot change your account or read your email. You can revoke it any time under Preferences → Security on Lichess, or by signing out here.
