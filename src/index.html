Sign in to play.
Instructions

1. Sign in
Signing in occurs on lichess.org. This site never sees your password or any of your account information. Lichess will ask you to allow this site to do the following things on your behalf:
- play games
- send/read/accept challenges
You can revoke this site's access at any time by clicking your username and then signing out.

2. Enable Voice Mode
Tap the 'Voice Mode' button. The page will ask for microphone permission the first time. This is enabled by going to Page Menu → Website Settings → Microphone → Allow.

3. Start a game
Choose your desired time control, and rated/casual. To start a game against a random opponent, click "Find an opponent". You can also enter the "Opponent" menu to select one of the maia bots, or choose "Someone else" and enter a Lichess username to issue a challenge.

4. Speak your move
Speak your move by saying the starting and ending squares. Moves should be spoken using the NATO alphabet:
A = alpha, B = bravo, C = charlie, D = delta,
E = echo, F = foxtrot, G = golf, H = hotel
Examples:
- "echo 2 echo 4"
- "bravo 8 charlie 6"
- "echo 1 golf 1" (castle short)
- "echo 8 charlie 8" (castle long)
- "hotel 7 hotel 8" (automatic promotion of pawn to a queen)
- "alpha 2 alpha 1 equals knight" (underpromotion to a knight)
Do not say any piece names. Do not say "takes" or "captures". Do not say "check" or "checkmate". Do not say "castles kingside/short" or "castles queenside/long". None of these words are accepted.

5. Verbal commands
"repeat" — Say the last move again.
"resign" — Resign the game? Player is asked for yes/no confirmation.
"draw" — Offer a draw? Player is asked for yes/no confirmation.
"cancel" — Drops an open yes/no question.
"clock" or "time" — Say the time remaining for each player.
"flip clock" — Flip which side of the screen your clock is on (in "Clock Mode").
"memo" — Transcribe a memo into the log. Useful for reporting problems. A pause stops the memo transcription.

6. The buttons
Voice Mode — Microphone and speech on or off.
Settings — Described below.
Clock — Enters full-screen "Clock Mode": a black screen showing just the two clocks, with the side to move shown in bolder font. Tap screen anywhere to exit.
Log — Everything heard and done. "Copy" puts the full text on the clipboard.
Practice — Understands moves and answers and gives a random legal reply, sending nothing to Lichess. Useful for practicing phrasings.

7. Settings
Show ratings — Display the player and opponent ratings.
Moves spoken — Choose how the opponent's moves are spoken to you: Piece ("knight charlie 6", "queen takes delta 5 check") or Squares ("bravo 8 charlie 6", "delta 8 delta 5").
Confirm — Choose how your move is confirmed: Chime, Voice, or None.

8. Tap to move
In addition to voice input, you can also input your move by tapping the starting and ending squares on the board. Do not drag the piece, only tapping is acceptable. This is useful for making quick moves if you get low on time, since entering moves by voice is much slower.

9. Select a better voice
The page speaks using your device's system voice. If you are using an iPhone or iPad, you can get a better voice by going to: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English. Choose a Premium or Enhanced voice (Ava is a good voice).
