Sign in to play.
Instructions

1. Sign In
Tap "Sign in with Lichess". Signing in happens on lichess.org, so this site never sees your password. Lichess asks you to let this site:
- play your games
- send challenges (to the maia bots or another player)
The site never accepts challenges: ones sent to you are ignored here, and you can answer them on Lichess.
To sign out, tap your name, then tap "Sign out?". This also ends the site's access on Lichess.

2. Choose How to Play
In Settings, set "Play by" to one of three modes:
Voice — Speak your moves (see 4 and 5), and the page speaks back: your opponent's moves, offers and the result. Your own move is confirmed as the Confirm setting says. Tap the Microphone button to turn the microphone on; tap again to turn it off. If necessary, you can turn on microphone access in Safari with Page Menu → Website Settings → Microphone → Allow.
Hybrid — Type your moves on a hardware keyboard (see 6 and 7), and the page speaks back just as in Voice Mode. What it says is also printed beside the board, under what you type, until the next move; a question stays until you answer it. Clock View (see 9) shows just the clocks. The page can only make sound after you tap it or press a key, so do one before your opponent's first move (tapping Clock is enough).
Keyboard — Type your moves, in silence: nothing is spoken and nothing chimes. Your opponent's moves, offers and the result are shown under your clock instead. In Clock View, each side's last move is shown under its clock, what you type under yours, and messages along the bottom.
In Hybrid and Keyboard Modes, the microphone stays off and the Microphone button is greyed out. In every mode, you can also tap a piece to move (see 8).

3. Start a Game
Pick a time control, or type your own into Custom as minutes+increment (for example 20+10), and choose Casual or Rated. Then either:
- Tap "Find an opponent" to play a random player. Rapid and longer games are accepted.
- Pick a maia bot under "Opponent", or "Someone else" and type a Lichess name, choose your color, and tap "Challenge". Blitz and longer games are accepted. If the challenge is declined, the page says so.
A game you start in the Lichess app or on lichess.org is joined here automatically, unless it is too fast for this site.
While a game is on, or a seek or challenge is waiting, the screen stays awake, in Board View and Clock View alike (see 9). If the connection to Lichess drops, the page opens it again by itself, and says "reconnected", with any move your opponent made in the meantime.

4. Speak Your Move (Voice Mode)
With the microphone on (see 2), say the starting square and then the ending square, using the NATO alphabet for the letters:
A = alpha
B = bravo
C = charlie
D = delta
E = echo
F = foxtrot
G = golf
H = hotel
Examples:
- "echo 2 echo 4"
- "bravo 8 charlie 6"
- "echo 1 golf 1" (white castles short)
- "echo 8 charlie 8" (black castles long)
- "hotel 7 hotel 8" (a pawn reaching the last rank becomes a queen)
- "alpha 2 alpha 1 equals knight" (to promote to anything else, say "equals" and the piece)
Say only the squares: no piece names (except after "equals"), and not "takes", "check" or "castles". A move with any of these words in it isn't accepted.
If the page didn't catch a move, it says "Say again." If it heard a move clearly but the move isn't legal, it says "That is not a legal move."

5. Voice Commands
"repeat" — Say the last move again.
"time" — Say the time left for each player.
"resign" — Asks "Resign the game?"
"draw" — Asks "Offer a draw?"
"yes" / "no" — Answer a question: your own resign or draw, your opponent's draw offer or takeback request, or claiming the win when your opponent has left.
"cancel" — Same as "no".
"flip" — Swap which side of Clock View your clock is on.
"memo" — Say "memo" and then a comment, in one breath, and the comment is written into the log. Useful for reporting problems.

6. Type Your Move (Hybrid and Keyboard Modes)
Type the move's two squares, then press Enter. Each file can be a letter or a number (a=1 to h=8), and the two can be mixed (h283 is h2h3):
- e2e4 or 5254
- g1f3 or 7163
- e1g1 or 5171 (white castles short)
- e7e8 or 5758 (a pawn reaching the last rank becomes a queen)
- e7e8n or 57584 (to promote to anything else, add q, r, b or n, or 1, 2, 3 or 4)
Entries are four characters long; a fifth is only taken when the move is a promotion. A key that can't go in the next place, such as a letter where a rank belongs, is ignored. Backspace deletes a character, Escape clears.
An entry that can't be played is cleared, so you type the next one from scratch. What you typed and the reason show in red until your next key. In Hybrid the reason is also spoken: "That is not a legal move.", or "Enter again." for an unfinished entry.
Typing into the Custom time or Lichess name box goes to that box, not the game: tap an empty part of the page first.

7. Typed Commands
Each is ended with Enter, like a move.
91 — Asks "Resign the game?"
92 — Asks "Offer a draw?"
93 — Repeat the last move.
94 — The time left for each player.
95 — Flip: swap which side of Clock View your clock is on.
1 / 0 — Yes / no: to your own question, your opponent's draw offer or takeback request, or claiming the win when your opponent has left. While a question is open, it must be answered before you can move.

8. Tap To Move
You can also move by tapping the piece and then the square it goes to. Don't drag the piece. A pawn tapped to the last rank always becomes a queen; to promote to anything else, say or type the move. Tapping is quicker than speaking or typing, which helps when you are low on time.

9. The Buttons
Microphone — Turns the microphone on or off (Voice Mode only).
Settings — Opens the Settings menu, described below.
Clock — Switches from Board View, the ordinary page with the board and these buttons, to Clock View: a black screen with just the two clocks, the side to move in bolder digits (Keyboard Mode adds the moves and messages: see 2). The screen stays awake while it is up. Tap anywhere to go back to Board View.
Log — Everything heard and done, kept across reloads. "Copy" puts the full text on the clipboard; "Clear" empties it. Tap anywhere else to close it.
Practice — A game as white against random legal moves, with nothing sent to Lichess. Useful for practicing moves and commands. Finding an opponent and challenging are switched off until you tap Practice again.
Your name — The account button. Tap it, then tap "Sign out?" to sign out.

10. Settings
Tap Settings to open the menu. Tap anywhere outside it, or press Escape, to close it.
Play by — Voice, Hybrid or Keyboard Mode (see 2).
Show ratings — Show player ratings beside the names.
Moves spoken — How your opponent's moves are spoken to you: Squares ("bravo 8, charlie 6", "delta 8, delta 5, check") or Pieces ("knight charlie 6", "queen takes delta 5, check").
Confirm — How your own move is confirmed: a chime (quiet, standard or loud), Voice, or None.
Keyboard Mode greys out Moves spoken and Confirm, since nothing is spoken.

11. Select a Better Voice
The page speaks (in Voice and Hybrid Modes) using your device's system voice. On an iPhone or iPad, you can get a better voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English. Choose a Premium or Enhanced voice (Ava is a good one).
